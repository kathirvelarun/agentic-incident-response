AIR - Agentic Incident Response

High-Level Architecture

AIR AI SRE is not a single LLM agent.

It is a multi-agent investigation platform that orchestrates multiple specialised agents.

Instead of asking an LLM:

"Find the root cause."

It performs something closer to:

Workflow Steps

  1. Alert Arrives
  1. Planner Agent
  1. Parallel Evidence Collection
  1. Data Normalization
  1. Correlation Agent
  1. Hypothesis Generation
  1. Specialized Investigation Agents
  1. Evidence Scoring
  1. Refinement Loop
  1. Historical Knowledge
  1. Causal Timeline
  1. Root Cause Ranking
  1. Recommendation Agent

Executive Summary

AIR is an autonomous AI SRE platform that investigates production incidents from alerts, gathers evidence across observability and engineering systems, formulates competing hypotheses, validates them through parallel agents, identifies the most probable root cause, recommends remediation, and continuously learns from incident outcomes.

Unlike traditional AIOps solutions that rely on predefined rules, AIR reasons over production context using agentic workflows, evidence correlation, historical incidents, and LLM-guided investigation.

Product Roadmap

Phase 1 (MVP): Alert intake, planner, evidence collection, RCA generation, Slack updates, ServiceNow integration, human approval.

Phase 2: Multi-hypothesis investigation, causal dependency graph, historical incident learning, evaluation framework, richer observability integrations.

Phase 3: Autonomous remediation with approval policies, deployment monitoring, proactive anomaly detection, continuous background agents, and production knowledge graph.
